Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Mindrop helps you save small details and find them in Spotlight. This policy explains how the app and this website handle information. Mindrop is provided by Fatih Saglam.

1. Information you save

The app stores the keywords, values, categories, dates, and preferences needed to provide its features on your device. You choose what to save, including any personal or sensitive information. No Mindrop account is required. The developer does not operate a server that receives or stores your memories and cannot access your private iCloud database.

2. iCloud sync

iCloud Sync is enabled by default in Mindrop. When available, it synchronizes your memories through Apple’s CloudKit private database associated with your iCloud account. Keywords, values, categories, and category sources use CloudKit encrypted fields; dates, identifiers, and settings such as the Sensitive flag are stored as record metadata. Apple provides the iCloud service under its own privacy policy and terms.

You can turn off iCloud Sync in Mindrop’s Settings. This stops syncing; it does not delete memories already stored locally or in iCloud. Signing out of iCloud keeps local memories. Signing into a different iCloud account can upload the local memories to that account when sync is enabled.

3. Spotlight and sensitive memories

Mindrop sends active memories to Apple’s on-device Spotlight index so you can find them in system search. Ordinary memories may show both their keyword and value in search results. Anyone able to use search on your device may be able to see them.

When you mark a memory Sensitive, its value is excluded from Spotlight; the keyword remains searchable. The app hides the value until device authentication succeeds, using Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode as available. Mindrop does not receive biometric data. If your device has no passcode, authentication cannot protect the value. Sensitive is a visibility and access control feature, not a separate encrypted vault.

4. Photos, camera, and on-device processing

When you choose to scan text or QR codes, or select a photo or shared image, Mindrop uses Apple’s on-device recognition tools to extract text. The selected text is stored when you save it; the source image is not stored as part of a memory. Camera access is requested when needed. Mindrop does not upload these images to the developer or an external recognition service.

On supported devices, Apple Intelligence can suggest categories using an on-device model. Your memory’s keyword and value may be processed locally for this purpose, including memories marked Sensitive. Mindrop does not send them to an external AI service.

5. System integrations

Share extensions, Siri, Shortcuts, and system controls pass information you choose to save into Mindrop. The Apple Watch app transfers new memories to your paired iPhone through Apple’s WatchConnectivity service and, when iCloud sync is enabled and available, CloudKit. A pending transfer may remain in a local outbox on the watch until delivery. Copying a value puts it on the system clipboard; opening a saved link, address, or phone number passes the relevant information to the app handling that action.

6. Analytics, advertising, and diagnostics

Mindrop includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. Apple may process platform diagnostics or make crash reports available under your device’s analytics settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

7. Retention and deletion

Memories without an expiry remain until you delete them. Deleting a memory removes it from the active list and Spotlight and moves it to Recently Deleted for up to 30 days. You can recover it or choose Delete Now to delete it permanently sooner. Expired memories leave Spotlight and remain recoverable for 7 days. Automatic permanent deletion is performed when Mindrop runs its cleanup, including at launch and when returning to the foreground.

Permanent deletions are sent to iCloud when sync is enabled and available. Devices that are offline or have sync disabled may retain copies until they synchronize. Turning sync off or uninstalling the app does not itself delete iCloud copies. Apple manages retention in its services and device backups under its own policies.

8. Support messages

If you contact mfsaglam1@icloud.com, the developer receives your email address and anything you include. This information is used to respond, troubleshoot, and follow up on your request. Keep sensitive saved values out of support messages. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. You can request its deletion using the same address.

9. This website

This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies, or contact forms. All visual assets are served with the site. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure it. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following external links or sending email is subject to the relevant provider’s policies.

10. Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete memories, manage Sensitive and expiry settings, disable iCloud sync, and manage device permissions. The developer cannot read or delete memories in your local storage or private iCloud account on your behalf. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, and to contact your local data protection authority. For information the developer holds directly, such as support correspondence, contact the address below.

11. Children and changes to this policy

Mindrop does not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-operated services. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, please contact us. This policy may be updated as the app changes; the date above identifies the latest version.
