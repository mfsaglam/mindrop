Common questions

How do I find a saved memory?

Save a keyword and a value in Mindrop. Swipe down on the Home Screen to open Spotlight and search for that keyword. You can also search inside Mindrop. Open a memory and tap the value to copy it.

Why isn’t my memory showing in Spotlight?

Open Mindrop to let it update the search index. Check that the memory has not expired or moved to Recently Deleted. In your iPhone’s Settings, check the Search settings for Mindrop and allow its content to appear. The exact setting names vary by iOS version. Spotlight may take some time to update.

Why does a result show a lock instead of the value?

The memory is marked Sensitive. Its keyword stays searchable, but its value stays out of Spotlight. Open the result in Mindrop and authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode to reveal it. Set a device passcode to use this protection.

How do I sync between my devices?

Use the same iCloud account on your devices and enable iCloud Sync in Mindrop’s Settings. Sync needs an available iCloud account and a network connection. Open the app on each device and allow time for changes to arrive. Turning sync off leaves existing copies in place.

Can I recover a deleted or expired memory?

Deleted memories can be recovered from Recently Deleted for up to 30 days, unless you used Delete Now. Expired memories stay recoverable in the Expired section for 7 days. After permanent deletion, Mindrop cannot recover them.

Can I save without opening Mindrop first?

Use Share in another app and choose Mindrop. If it is missing, tap More in the share sheet. You can also use Siri, Shortcuts, a Control Center or Lock Screen control, an Action Button shortcut on supported iPhones, or the Apple Watch app.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. Saving and finding memories work without it. Automatic categorization uses an on-device Apple Intelligence model when available. You can choose a category yourself.
